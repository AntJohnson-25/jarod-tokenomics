AI API Key (Anthropic or OpenAI)
The "Validate with Claude" feature on the Onboarding tab sends your onboarding plan to Anthropic's or OpenAI's API (auto-detected from your key) for an AI review. This uses your own account and bills to you. Your key is stored only in this browser and is sent only to api.anthropic.com (sk-ant- keys) or api.openai.com (other sk- keys). It also powers the live Refresh Pricing search. All other features work without a key.
